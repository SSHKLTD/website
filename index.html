About us

To reinterpret social media marketing through the power of narrative — and to inspire the greatness in every audience.

Founded in 2010, Photoblog.hk grew into one of Hong Kong's most popular photography communities, with over 400,000 followers. In 2017 it evolved into Social Strategy Hong Kong, bringing professional social media marketing and crisis management to higher education, public utilities and commercial brands.

Our team blends journalist instincts with creative execution — we read the news cycle, plan ahead of it, and tell brand stories that cut through.

  • B/03
