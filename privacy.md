# Privacy Policy

What we collect when you get in touch through this website, why, and how to ask us to change or delete it.

## Who we are

This website, www.sshk.ltd, is run by Social Strategy Hong Kong (SSHK), 6/F, China Paint Building, 1163 Canton Road, Mong Kok, Hong Kong. We handle personal data in line with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong.

## What we collect

- **When you contact us.** The brief form on our contact page does not store anything on this website: it opens an email in your own mail app, addressed to info@sshk.ltd. If you send it, we receive what you typed: your name, email address, phone number (optional) and message. The same applies when you email or phone us directly, or send us a CV.
- **When you browse.** We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies, and collects information like your device, browser, approximate location and pages viewed. Our hosting provider also keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) to run and protect the site.

## How we use it

- To reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal and work with you if you become a client.
- To consider job and internship applications.
- To understand and improve how this website works.

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it with service providers that help us run our email, website and analytics, or where the law requires us to.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and application emails only for as long as we need them for the purposes above, then delete them.

## Your choices and rights

Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance you can ask to access or correct the personal data we hold about you. You can also ask us to delete it, or to stop contacting you. Email [info@sshk.ltd](mailto:info@sshk.ltd) or call [+852 2713 0997](tel:+85227130997) and we will reply within a reasonable time.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s opt-out tool for Google Analytics, without affecting how the site works for you.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The latest version will always be here.
