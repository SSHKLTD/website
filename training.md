THE PROGRAMMES

## Three things we teach best.

PROGRAMME 01

### Crisis Management & Social Listening

THE WAR-ROOM CURRICULUM

Where negative sentiment starts — unfavourable news, media pile-ons, influencer comments, netizen attacks — and the counter-move for each: internal alignment and risk assessment, lines-to-take and press-release optimisation, alternative-opinion development, information penetration and sentiment management. Closed with dos-and-don'ts drills on real Hong Kong cases.

LINE-TO-TAKE DRILLS · LIVE CASE CLINICS · DOS & DON'TS

PROGRAMME 02

### Social Media Strategy & Platforms

THE 2027 PLAYBOOK

How Hong Kong actually decides now: the new consumer journey, platform by platform — Threads culture, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, Google Reviews. The death of organic reach, the KPI shift from followers to conversions, and a micro-influencer selection framework that survives budget season.

PLATFORM PLAYBOOK · NEW KPIs · MICRO-KOL FRAMEWORK

PROGRAMME 03

### AI Workflow for Comms Teams

FROM MONITORING TO ACTION

How we run AI in production: crawlers on five-minute intervals, AI categorisation across millions of posts a day, 24/7 WhatsApp alerts and an ask-anything chatbot on top. Then the hands-on part — wiring the same monitoring-to-action loop, plus AI-assisted content and reporting, into your own team.

AI MONITORING PIPELINE · ALERT DESIGN · HANDS-ON WORKFLOW
