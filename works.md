02 / 22 · TRANSPORT & AVIATION **Cathay Pacific**
Since 2015, we've been the desk that watches what Hong Kong says about its airline — monitoring, ePR consultancy and content management, nine years without a handover.

03 / 22 · TRANSPORT & AVIATION **Hong Kong International Airport**
From route reveals to a race on the new third runway, our retainer for AAHK turns aviation infrastructure into stories passengers actually share — without ever compromising the operational voice.

04 / 22 · TRANSPORT & AVIATION **MTR**
Content calendars mapped to the operating year, service moments translated into plain bilingual posts, and same-day responses when the network is in the news — a retainer built for four million daily journeys.

05 / 22 · PROPERTY & RETAIL **New World Group**
Conglomerate social usually splinters. We built New World a shared tone and design grammar, then ran the calendar across its brands so the group reads as one company with many doors.

06 / 22 · COMMERCIAL **Alibaba**
B2B never means Boring2Business. We wrote for the operator, not the org chart — recognisable trading situations, dry humour where it earned its place, and platform-native formats that made sourcing content watchable.

07 / 22 · EDUCATION **Van Cleef & Arpels · L’ÉCOLE**
High jewellery education is a niche within a niche. Seven years on L'ÉCOLE's APAC channels, we made scholarship scroll-worthy — exhibition campaigns, craft stories and a tone as precise as the ateliers.

08 / 22 · ENTERTAINMENT & EVENTS **AsiaWorld-Expo**
A social retainer that ran the venue as a publisher — one recognisable voice underneath, a dedicated content lane for every show on top, momentum kept up between headline events.

09 / 22 · EDUCATION **HKUST**
We matched HKUST to a matrix of KOLs and KOCs, then measured what matters: every single creator outperformed their own average, with uplift — not borrowed reach — as the reported number.

10 / 22 · ENTERTAINMENT & EVENTS **BOCHK Hong Kong Tennis Open**
We ran every day of the Tennis Open across five platforms — live match content, player moments, ticket pushes — and closed the tournament 24% over its content target with 8.94M reach.

11 / 22 · TRANSPORT & AVIATION **Hong Kong Tramways**
We gave the ding ding a format as charming as the ride — sixteen branching films on YouTube where every click steers the journey, proof that heritage brands can lead on format.

12 / 22 · ENTERTAINMENT & EVENTS **The North Face 100 Hong Kong**
A hundred kilometres needs more than a highlights reel. For TNF100 we built the whole arc — theme film, athlete stories, countdown — content that respects the trail community it speaks to.

13 / 22 · PROPERTY & RETAIL **Arcade @ Cyberport**
A mall inside a tech park needs its own rhythm. Our retainer built one — recurring pillars like Wellness Wednesday, tenant stories, and issue monitoring that kept small issues small.

14 / 22 · PROPERTY & RETAIL **Chun Wo**
A contractor's brand problem is invisibility. Dream Girls solved it with real professionals — a Chun Wo-led girl group of engineers and surveyors from across the industry, whose songs, shoots and appearances have carried the story since 2020.

15 / 22 · BANKING & FINANCE **OCBC**
We run OCBC's social the way good bankers talk to clients: clearly. Real situations instead of product sheets, explainers without jargon, and design consistent enough to be recognised without the logo.

16 / 22 · BANKING & FINANCE **Generali**
Insurance is a grudge topic; mascots aren't. We gave Generali a friendlier cast and lighter storytelling that earn attention between campaigns, so promotional pushes land with an audience already listening.

17 / 22 · PROPERTY & RETAIL **Taikoo Place**
We repositioned Taikoo Place from office address to destination — two content pillars, one always-on calendar, and a community of workers who became the campaign's recurring cast.

18 / 22 · PROPERTY & RETAIL **MTR Malls · PopCorn**
We ran PopCorn's anniversary as one long celebration — and the numbers held: double-digit engagement rates on Facebook and Instagram alike, rare air for shopping-mall social.

19 / 22 · PROPERTY & RETAIL **TSL Jewellery**
Relaunching a listed jeweller on social meant choosing one thing to own. We chose weddings — an expert voice, a rebuilt feed, and a brand that finally photographed as well as its rings.

20 / 22 · NGO & COMMUNITY **UNICEF HK**
Content and fundraising work for UNICEF HK, headlined by a WhatsApp sticker pack — small, shareable, and forwarded through the exact family groups donations come from.

21 / 22 · TRANSPORT & AVIATION **YunExpress**
Logistics B2B usually means brochures. For YunExpress we shot the leadership instead — C-level interview films across four platforms, cut into shorts, turning corporate positioning into watchable content.

22 / 22 · GOVERNMENT & PUBLIC **John Tsang 2017 Campaign**
